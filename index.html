Formed in the Pacific Northwest, I study courses from Peterson Academy and rebuild them into an overview video, a slide deck, a deep-dive podcast, and a study guide for each one. This site documents my growth and development in becoming a well-educated, productive individual who works to foster the well-being of society.

Studying with us assumes your own Peterson Academy enrollment. We don’t redistribute their transcripts or videos — make your own instead.
